## Less than three minutes with a physician.

That is how long a pharma sales rep has. Within that short window, the rep must present
FDA-regulated efficacy, dosing, and safety data via an iPad app built on Veeva's
proprietary platform and subject to BMS medical-legal review. Every therapeutic brand
faced the same challenges: bespoke IVAs, long timelines, high costs, and the need to
retrain reps before every launch. The brief: a single global design system and templates
that made every brand's IVA faster to build, easier to use, and compliant by default.

## My role

I led UX and UI for the future-state IVA system — the template architecture, interaction
patterns, and per-brand style application — and owned storyboarding and QA passes that
carried each IVA through medical-legal approval. The system was adopted across 10+
Bristol Myers Squibb brands.

## The system: one template language for every brand

The system standardised what should never be redesigned twice — global navigation, slide
templates, a typography scale, button behaviour — and left brand expression (palette,
imagery, iconography) as the variable layer. A standardised story flow shaped the sitemap;
templates accommodated every content type from hero imagery to dense clinical data; the
type scale kept legibility on a device read at arm's length across a desk.

## Regulatory UX: the interactions that matter

Compliance was designed as interaction, not decoration: an Important Safety Information
tray available from every slide; a forced flow that requires scrolling the full ISI before
the Proceed button activates; a zoom overlay for clinical charts; and a global references
slide so citations never crowd the content. These patterns passed medical-legal review
once — then every brand inherited them.

## From storyboard to sales floor

Every IVA shipped with a storyboard — sitemap, linear screen captures, functional and
animation notes — used twice: by clients for approval, and by sales reps to rehearse
before walking into a physician's office. Multiple QA and stakeholder testing rounds
preceded every launch.
