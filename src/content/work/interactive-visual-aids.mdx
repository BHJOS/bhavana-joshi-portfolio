## Less than three minutes with a physician.

That is how long a pharma sales rep has. Within that short window, the rep must present
FDA-regulated efficacy, dosing, and safety data via an iPad app built on Veeva's
proprietary platform and subject to BMS medical-legal review. Every therapeutic brand
faced the same challenges of bespoke IVAs, long timelines, high costs, and the need to
retrain reps before every launch. The brief was a single global design system and templates
that made every brand's IVA faster to build, easier to use, and compliant by default.

## My role

I was part of the core team that helped define the UX and UI for the future-state IVA
system, including the template architecture, interaction patterns, and per-brand style
application. I also owned the storyboarding and the QA pass that guided each IVA through
medical-legal approval. The system was adopted across 10+ Bristol Myers Squibb brands.

## One template language for every brand

The system standardized global navigation, slide templates, a typographic scale, and
button behavior, leaving brand expression (palette, imagery, iconography) as the
variable layer. A standardized story flow shaped the sitemap. Templates accommodated
every content type, from hero imagery to dense clinical data, and the typographic scale
kept text legible on a device read at arm's length across a desk.

## Regulatory UX, the interactions that matter

Compliance was designed for interaction, not decoration. The Important Safety Information
tray was available on every slide, with a forced flow that required scrolling through the
full ISI before the Proceed button was activated. A zoom overlay for clinical charts and a
global references slide ensured citations never crowded the content. These patterns passed
medical-legal review once, and then every brand inherited them.

## From storyboard to sales floor

Every IVA was shipped with a storyboard sitemap, linear screen captures, functional and
animation notes, and was reviewed by clients for approval and by sales reps to rehearse
before walking into a physician's office. Multiple rounds of QA and stakeholder testing
preceded every launch.
