At the beginning of 2010, and after eight years of planning, the West Windsor township
finally agreed to lease the old firehouse to the local arts council to convert it into an
Arts Center. While the eight-month-long renovation was on, preparations started for a
successful launch. As the marketing committee chair, I led the brand design and marketing
campaign.

## Challenge

West Windsor is a township in New Jersey with a population of 28,074 — ethnically diverse
and affluent. Other than highly rated school districts, there isn't much to do in West
Windsor save for a couple of restaurants, malls, and parks in neighbouring towns. The
local arts council hoped to inject some excitement with a diverse offering of art classes,
musical performances, dance, and art exhibitions.

The building was situated on a quiet street that people would often overlook on their way
to the train station or the strip mall nearby. As a volunteer-run organisation, there were
limited funds available to promote the organisation and garner interest prior to the
launch.

### Insights

- The target audience was primarily composed of seniors or adults.
- Only a small group of people were familiar with the Arts Council.
- This lack of awareness made fundraising a challenge.
- Without a home of their own, the arts council was forced to host small-scale events at
  the local library or a local park, thereby reducing attendance.

### Signs

As part of a teaser campaign, our team proposed painting murals on some of the nearby
boarded storefronts. However, the township denied this request. The next option was to use
the windows of the firehouse to draw the attention of passers-by. As renovations
progressed, the new signs in the window started to attract more attention.

## Key Decisions

**Turning a rejection into the campaign's best asset.** When the township denied the mural
proposal, the firehouse windows became the canvas instead. The signs sat directly on the
route people already walked to the train station, so the renovation itself became the
advertising.

**Shipping an interim page rather than waiting for the real site.** A Dreamweaver
placeholder kept visitors engaged with updates for months while the full site was still
being designed — awareness started building before launch instead of at it.

**Building the campaign on free tiers.** Constant Contact's free plan covered 1,000
subscribers and a new Facebook page cost nothing. For a volunteer-run organisation with no
promotional budget, the constraint set the channel strategy.

outreach events and rental space, select performances in theater, music, and dance,
literary events, and art exhibitions.
